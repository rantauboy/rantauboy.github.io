🎙️ Tixi-Voice - Talk Freely, Even Fully Offline

Tixi Voice is a speech studio for your own computer. It reads text out loud in Persian and English, it turns recordings into written text, and it can speak in a voice that sounds like a person you choose. Everything happens on your machine. Nothing is sent to the internet, and you never create an account.

⬇️ Get Tixi-Voice for Windows

Free and open source. Portable build for Windows x64 — nothing to install.

💡 What Is Tixi-Voice?

Most voice tools ask you to upload your files to a website and wait. Tixi Voice works the other way around. It is a single folder that lives on your Windows computer and uses every part of your PC to do the work — no uploads, no waiting in a queue, no monthly fee.

The application is built around three well-known open speech engines. Piper handles text-to-speech, so written words come out as natural spoken audio. Whisper handles speech-to-text, so an audio file becomes a text file you can copy, edit and search. OpenVoice lets you capture the character of a voice and apply it to new speech — your own personal voice.

Because the whole program is self-contained, you can copy the folder to a USB stick, move it to another Windows machine, and carry on exactly where you left off. There is no installer, no background service and no leftover files scattered across your system.

✨ What You Can Do With It

🗣️ Read text aloud

Paste a paragraph, an article or a whole document and listen to it in a natural Persian or English voice. Adjust the speaking speed until it feels comfortable.

📝 Turn audio into text

Drop in a voice memo, an interview or a lecture recording and get a written transcript you can copy straight into any editor.

🎭 Create a personal voice

Give the app a short sample of a voice, and it will read new text in that style. Great for narrations, learning material and accessibility projects.

🌐 Switch between two languages

Persian and English are both first-class citizens here. Move between them without changing any settings or downloading anything extra.

🔌 Stay disconnected

Disconnect the internet cable, switch off Wi-Fi, and the program keeps working exactly the same. Your voice notes never leave the room.

💾 Carry it anywhere

Run it from a USB drive on any compatible Windows computer. Your settings and history travel with the folder.

🖥️ What Your Computer Needs

  • Operating system: Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit (x64) edition.
  • Memory: 8 GB of RAM is a sensible minimum. 16 GB keeps longer transcripts and larger voices running comfortably.
  • Free disk space: roughly 4–6 GB, depending on which voice models you keep on hand.
  • Processor: any modern Intel or AMD 64-bit processor. A dedicated graphics card is nice to have but not required.
  • Sound: any speakers or headphones, plus a microphone or audio file if you plan to transcribe.

No Python installation, no drivers, no extra downloads. The folder contains everything the program needs to run.

🚀 Your First Session, Step by Step

Making speech from text

Open the text-to-speech area of the window, paste or type your text into the large box, choose Persian or English, and select a voice. Press the play button to hear a preview, then export the result as an audio file when you are happy with it. The voice list will show everything already available in the folder.

Turning a recording into text

Switch to the speech-to-text area, pick the audio file you want transcribed, and confirm the language. The transcript appears in the window as it is produced. When it finishes, copy the text out or save it as a plain text file. Clear speech and a quiet room give noticeably better results.

Building a personal voice

In the voice section, load a short, clean recording of the voice you want to use — around half a minute of steady speech is plenty. Save the profile with a name you recognise, then select it whenever you generate new speech. Keep the sample free of background music and noise for the most convincing result.

🔒 Your Voice Stays Yours

Every calculation happens on your own hardware. Text you type, audio you import and voices you create are stored in the application folder on your computer and nowhere else. There is no telemetry, no analytics endpoint and no cloud account. If you work with sensitive material — medical notes, legal drafts, interviews, classroom recordings — the file never crosses your network connection.

This also means the program runs happily on a laptop with no connection at all: in a classroom, on a train, in a remote office, or on a machine that is deliberately kept offline for security reasons.

🧰 If Something Does Not Work

  • The window does not open. Confirm you are on a 64-bit Windows system and that you extracted the archive fully before double-clicking. Running the application from inside a compressed folder will not work.
  • Windows blocks the file. Right-click the application file, open Properties, and tick Unblock at the bottom of the General tab, then try again.
  • Speech generation is slow. Close other heavy programs. The longer the text, the more work the processor has to do; splitting a long document into paragraphs keeps things responsive.
  • The transcript is inaccurate. Check that the selected language matches the recording, then try a cleaner sample. Background noise is the most common cause.
  • No sound comes out. Use the speaker test in Windows settings to confirm the correct output device is selected, then reopen the application.
